the `closed-operands-worst.json` source snapshot, not a subsequent fix.

## The host is already a rectangular box

For #286665, `IfcArbitraryClosedProfileDef` has exactly four indexed corners:
(-8,-1436), (8,-1436), (8,1436), (-8,1436), in millimetres. Its authored
extrusion is (0,0,-1), depth 3957.03058 mm. Other investigated members likewise
have 16 mm rectangular thickness and heights 2887, 2918, 2934 or 2947 mm.

There are no extra collinear points, parallelogram shears or trapezoids.
The maximum composed-frame orthogonality error across these hosts is
4.2842e-10, below `clean_box`'s 1e-9 gate. An affine host generalization is
therefore unnecessary for this family. A production refusal cannot be assigned
to the source polygon without measuring the processed profile or later gates.

## Closed output: the last three cuts were present

A subsequent `finite-closed-worst.json` result had no CSG failures and exact
closed edges, with volume 0.09055584536348411 m3. Its proximity to the oracle's
six-cutter prefix volume initially suggested missing final cutters. Explicit
host-frame +X ray intersections disprove that hypothesis:

|---|---|---|---:|

| 286734 | (-1.420,-0.020) | 0.007869187757 to 0.008006441473 | 0.007873310255 |

Every ray intersects the corresponding original finite cutter and no other
cutter. The large blind skins at the two negative-y corners survive. The
positive-y corner has a 15.24 micrometre native skin versus a 3.59 micrometre
authored skin. At that location the OCC material mesh has no intersection,
so OCC itself does not preserve this extremely thin skin; that oracle detail
must not be mistaken for evidence that the native cutter is absent.

The original stock's positive thin face is x=0.008 m. The native face sits near
0.00800644 m, approximately 6.44 micrometres outward. Both stock and finite
cutter faces are quantized by the coarse kernel lattice. Over the plate's
large surface area, micrometre face shifts materially affect signed volume
even when all edges are closed and every cutter exists.

The parent changed source evaluation to prefer fine work units (256, then 16)
before coarse unit work, keeping exact analytic clipping first. The original
2e-6 m3 assertion now passes (`fine-first-member-test.log`); its threshold was
not weakened. Independent ray evidence is recorded in
`member-286665-last-three-rays.json` and its reproducible script
`member-last-three-rays.py`. The ray conclusion is direct mesh/operand evidence;
the quantization explanation is supported by the measured shifted faces and
the fine-unit intervention. Subsequent full-model or packaged-viewer success
is a separate verification boundary.
